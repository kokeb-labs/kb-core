Detailed Description

Sleeps that wait at least the time asked, drift-free periodic waits, the tick and the monotonic clock.

  • SleepFor(Milliseconds) and SleepFor(Microseconds) block for at least the duration: it is converted to ticks rounded up, plus one tick for the partial tick in progress (docs/rtos-boundary.md).
  • Periodic wakes on a fixed grid (Wait(period)): no drift, and an overrun (the next wake already passed) is reported and counted instead of hidden.
  • Now() is the 64-bit monotonic clock in microseconds; TickRateHz() and ToTicksAtLeast() expose the tick.

Not callable from an interrupt handler.
