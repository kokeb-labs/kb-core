Detailed Description

template<typename Bus, MonotonicClock Clock>
requires (I2cBus<Bus> || SpiDevice<Bus>)
class kb::iface::AsyncFromSync< Bus, Clock >

The asynchronous contract over a synchronous port, for ports that have no native form yet (ADR-0015): Start() runs the whole transfer at once (bounded by the port's own timeout) and the next Poll() reports it. It is not a hot-path fix: kNonBlocking is false, and a handler that must not wait requires NonBlocking<Port>.

Deadlines are kept as a native port keeps them: a deadline already passed at Start() starts nothing (Timeout), and a transfer that ends after its deadline completes as Timeout.

Template Parameters
BusAn I2cBus or a SpiDevice.
ClockA MonotonicClock (the time a transfer ended).
