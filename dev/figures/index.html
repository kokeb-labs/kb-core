Figures¶

Every diagram of this site is a committed SVG pair, light and dark, drawn from a small Python module by the documentation toolchain (figkit, shipped in the shared kokeb-mkdocs-theme) and the kb-core kit on top of it (tools/docs/kbfig.py). There is no script in the reader's browser: the pages load no Mermaid, stay fast on a phone and print. The figure catalogue lists every figure with its description.

What every figure has¶

  • One source. docs/diagrams/figures/<id>.py is the only place a figure is edited. A figure that shows facts of the repository (the components, a rule file) reads them in data(), so it cannot drift from the code.
  • A light and a dark file. <id>.svg and <id>.dark.svg, laid out once, so the two cannot differ. Pages embed both with a marker pair and the site follows the theme toggle.
  • Accessible text. One sentence of alt becomes the <title> and the alt of every embed; the Reads: paragraph of the module docstring becomes the <desc> and the long description under the figure. Colour is never the only carrier of meaning: edges that are forbidden or faulty are dashed or labelled, states carry a text.
  • Contrast of at least 4.5:1 for every text, in both themes, checked as the figure is built, and text sizes that stay readable at README width and on a phone.
  • A freshness check. kokeb-docs figures --check fails CI when a committed SVG differs from a fresh build, and tools/docs/check_figures.py rejects Mermaid fences, .mmd files, a missing light or dark file or source, and an SVG with a script or an external reference.

The palette¶

Colours are tokens, never hex values: the Kokeb copper (gold in dark) for kb-core itself, and one tone for each kind of thing, the same in every figure, so a reader learns it once.

Role Tone Used for
core copper (gold in dark) kb-core as a whole, kb::utils
contract blue kb::interfaces, port contracts, shared state, inputs
handler violet kb::handlers, bindings, the start-up pipeline
rtos amber kb::rtos and its backends, timing
port teal MCU ports, the host port, bus domains
driver green driver-<chip> (and "ok", "allowed", outputs)
product magenta the application and the board HAL
soup slate, dashed when planned SOUP and things not built yet
bad rose forbidden edges, failures, fault paths
tool brown generators, the kb tool, tasks and tests

Solid arrows are calls or "depends on"; dashed arrows are optional, returns or forbidden (always with a label that says which). Cards have a coloured stripe and 10 px corners; regions are tinted bands with an upper-case label.

Templates¶

kbfig adds what figkit leaves to each figure:

Call Draws
kbfig.row(f, x, y, w, cards) Cards side by side, equal height, with a role colour each
kbfig.lane(f, x, y, w, label, cards, role=...) A tinted band with a label and a row of cards (a layer, a zone)
kbfig.flow(f, x, y, w, steps) A left to right pipeline of cards joined by arrows
kbfig.pairs(f, x, y, w, rows) Rows of two pills and an arrow with a note (allowed and forbidden edges)
kbfig.sequence(f, y, actors, messages) A sequence diagram: lifelines, calls, returns, self calls, notes
kbfig.loop(f, box, side, label) A self-transition on a state
kbfig.legend(*roles) A legend from role names

State machines are drawn with f.card per state on a grid and f.edge for each transition (see docs/diagrams/figures/handler-health.py); a layered stack with kbfig.lane per layer (layer-stack.py).

Add a figure¶

  1. Copy the closest module in docs/diagrams/figures/ to <id>.py (lower case, hyphens) and change its content. Keep a Reads: paragraph in the docstring, a one-sentence alt and a legend that names every colour you use.
  2. Build and look at it: kokeb-docs figures <id> (from the repository root, with KOKEB_DOCS_CONFIG=_config/mkdocs.yml) writes docs/diagrams/<id>.svg and <id>.dark.svg. The build refuses a figure with low contrast, text that does not fit, overlapping cards, an arrow under a card or a label on a card; fix the layout rather than the check. rsvg-convert -w 1200 docs/diagrams/<id>.svg -o /tmp/<id>.png gives a picture to open.
  3. Embed it in a page with a marker pair, and run kokeb-docs figures again to fill it:
<!-- figure: <id> -->
<!-- /figure -->
  1. Run kokeb-docs check, python3 tools/docs/check_figures.py and the strict site build, or tools/preflight.sh, which runs all three. Commit the module, the two SVGs, figures.json and diagrams/index.md together.

Rules of thumb: at most about ten cards per figure (split a bigger story in two), arrows only between neighbours or along a clear gap, labels of a few words (put detail in the card text), the same role colour for the same thing as everywhere else, and a figure that shows repository facts reads them in data() and names its sources.
